## Post 1: demo video (post after recording)

GRUDGE: a buyer agent for @virtuals_io ACP that hires on its own private memory, not the public score.

## Post 2: build log (post now, before the video)

Build log, GRUDGE for the @sibylcap hackathon.

Public agent reputation is one number everyone shares. We took a fresh provider to a public score of 100 on ERC-8004 with two $0.01 jobs from a wallet we control. That is the problem.

So the buyer keeps its own memory: a trust vector per counterparty (spec adherence, latency, refunds, price drift), learned only from jobs it ran, decayed over time. Who to hire, what terms, what price all come out of it. No memory, no decision: the broker exits.

Found the hard way: the sponsored @virtuals_io wallets reject Base Sepolia, so everything runs on @base mainnet at a cent a job. ERC-8004's getSummary reverts without a client list, which is the registry telling you trust is not a scalar.
